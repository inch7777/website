Today when I turned on the faucet, I noticed the red and blue signs separately on the two sides of the knob, representing hot and cold water. 

Suddenly I thought to myself: is blue intrinsically associated with coldness and red hotness, or it’s the case only because WE associate them together within most of the contexts? But then is it even possible to separate an object from all its context, like blue from all the objects having the form of blue? 

This thought enhanced my understanding of Wittgenstein’s TLP, in which the logical form of an object, and that how something is being SHOWN within its context rather than SAID directly, is discussed.
